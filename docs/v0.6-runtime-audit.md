## Documented versus actual guarantees

- Budget reservation is conservative: worst-case units are reserved and never refunded.
- Unknown provider usage is now represented as unknown rather than zero.
- The HTTP adapter is OpenAI-compatible only and unverified live.
- Timeout cancellation is cooperative; a late provider response may still incur cost.
- No model-output recovery or retry loop is implemented in the inference path.
- Cost units are abstract accounting units, not money.

## Prioritized gaps

1. Live-provider protocol and error behavior: high impact, unverified.
2. Unknown-cost semantics: high impact, fixed in V0.6.
3. Retry and escalation accounting: medium impact, documented as external/caller-driven.
4. Benchmark baselines and fairness: medium impact, implemented in V0.6.
5. CLI exposure: low impact, deferred.
6. Model-output recovery: low impact and out of scope for V0.6.
