## Known limitations at freeze

- Fixture generation is deterministic seed increments of one generator
  (generator familiarity risk).
- No distributions beyond IN_DISTRIBUTION were evaluated.
- No composition verification.
- Receipts are content-addressed only (no signatures).
- Database has a single schema version; no migration history.
- Drift detection covers evidence-family topology only.
