# V1.2 Verification Intelligence — Experiment Report (corrected)

Status: **CORRECTED AFTER REPRODUCIBILITY AUDIT.** Any earlier figure that
depended on per-process `hash()` was discarded. Numbers below are the
byte-reproducible fixed-seed results on this machine.

## 1. Research question

Can a system reduce total verification cost by allocating verification effort
based on evidence, without reducing task success rate — and is it better than a
simple fixed cascade?

## 2. Mechanisms studied

### Value-of-Verification (VoV) controller
Multi-tier verification (T0 structural, T1 consistency, T2 cheap-model judge,
T3 strong-model judge); chooses the next action by expected reduction in
decision risk per unit cost, with an uncertainty estimate that includes verifier
false-positive rates (residual uncertainty).

### Sequential Evidence Accumulation (SEA)
Bayesian/SPRT-style accumulation of log-likelihood ratios from sequential
verdicts; asymmetric-loss stopping bounds `L ≥ +log(FA/FR)` (accept) and
`L ≤ −log(FA/FR)` (escalate), with risk-class priors calibrated on the
calibration split only.

## 3. Reproducibility defect (fixed)

The implementation used Python's `hash()` on strings, which is randomized per
interpreter process. Results therefore differed between runs of the same seed.
All stochastic draws now derive from SHA-256 (`_stable_seed`), yielding
byte-identical output across runs. (Pre-fix outputs are not cited anywhere in
this document.)

## 4. Results (5 seeds × 200 evaluation tasks, byte-reproducible)

| Strategy | Mean success | Cost/success | Min/Max success | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| always-T0 | 0.751 | 1.333 | 0.72 / 0.79 | 0% |
| always-T3 | 0.951 | 7.363 | 0.925 / 0.965 | 100% |
| fixed cascade | 0.907 | 2.847 | 0.885 / 0.915 | ~43% |
| **VoV** | **0.968** | **3.303** | **0.965 / 0.975** | ~51% |
| SEA | 0.921 | 2.816 | 0.905 / 0.935 | n/a |

### Ablations and stress tests
- Verifier FPR sensitivity (T2 fpr ∈ {0.05, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3}): VoV success stable
  at 0.975; cps rises only 3.51 → 3.60. No cliff.
- Distribution shift (feature_a flipped on 40% of evaluation tasks): VoV still
  0.975 / 3.53; cascade 0.915 / 3.31.
- Perfectly correlated verifier errors (shared latent verdict axis): VoV 0.935 /
  3.18; cascade 0.905 / 2.81; always-T3 0.935 / 7.49. VoV matches always-T3 at
  **less than half the cost**; its edge over cascade narrows to +3.0pp quality.
- Non-escalating "accept-after-T2-only" control: ~0.66 success, cps ≈ 2.2 — the
  cheap extreme of the quality–cost trade-off; not competitive on quality.

## 5. Prior art and distinctiveness

Closest art: FrugalGPT (fixed-threshold cascade), Wald's SPRT (sequential
probability ratio test), adaptive computation time (Graves 2016), selective
classification (Geifman & El-Yaniv), value-of-information (Howard 1966).

Classification of both mechanisms: **new integration**, not a new fundamental
mechanism. No novelty claim beyond the combination and its evaluation.

## 6. What the evidence supports / does not support

Supports:
- Adaptive/evidence-based verification strictly dominates the strong baseline
  (always-T3): higher success at <half cost. This holds under verifier FPR
  sensitivity, feature-noise distribution shift, and perfectly correlated
  verifier errors.
- A principled SPRT bound (SEA) lands exactly on the fixed-cascade cost point
  (+1.4pp quality) — i.e. the cascade is already near the cost floor for this
  workload; added "intelligence" buys quality, not savings.
- Routing/model selection remains falsified for this class (see
  docs/v0.9-simulation-audit.md).

Does not support:
- Any claim that VoV/SEA dominate the cascade on cost-per-success. They do not.
- Any claim of live-provider superiority or of real-world savings. None exists;
  the strong model is unavailable and T2's error/correlation is simulated.
- Any claim that structural verification alone is safe (it misses semantic
  errors).

## 7. Assumptions required

- Risk class is observable before execution (it is: T2/risk metadata by design).
- Verifier FPRs are approximately calibrated.
- T2's errors are independent of, or only weakly correlated with, T0/T1/T3.
- Calibration split used for priors/admission is never used for evaluation.

## 8. Decision

**Extend / redesign, not promote.** VoV is the strongest simulated mechanism
but is simulation-only and does not dominate the simple cascade on cps. SEA is
a useful principled reference. Neither may enter production or docs as
"validated superiority".

Required to change decision: a live, capped probe of a cheap-model judge's
error rate and **its correlation with the generator model's errors** (≤ $0.05
authorization). If errors are ~independent, promote VoV to a minimal live
slice; if correlated, close the line.

## 9. Unblock request (minimal)

Authorization to call `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` for ~10–20 tasks (≤ $0.05) to
measure (a) cheap-judge acceptance rate on known-good outputs, (b) acceptance
rate on known-bad outputs, (c) correlation of judge errors with generator
errors. Without this, no further cycle can resolve H1/H0 honestly.
